Loam privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Who uses Loam

Loam is a personal tool with a single user, its owner. It is not offered to the public.

What Loam accesses

With the owner's consent, Loam requests read-only Google scopes: Gmail (read only), Calendar (read only), Contacts and other contacts (read only), Drive (read only), and the signed-in email address. It cannot send, change or delete anything.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is read only by the owner's own copy of Loam, running on the owner's own computer, to let the owner search and recall their own information.
  • Data is stored locally on that computer. Backups are encrypted.
  • Sign-in credentials are kept in the computer's system keychain.
  • Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to third parties, and nothing is used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Loam's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
